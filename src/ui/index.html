Proxy Limits
seconds

Hard ceiling on how long Gatehouse will wait for an upstream response. Agent-requested timeouts are clamped to this value. Range: 1 second to 30 minutes.

MiB

Upstream responses larger than this are rejected with a 502. Increase for endpoints that legitimately return large payloads (file fetches, audio transcription, image generation). Range: 1 to 100 MiB.

SSO / OAuth Configuration

Enable for IdPs that don't emit email_verified (e.g. PocketID). Leave off for IdPs that DO emit it (Google, Authentik, Keycloak), since the strict check is your safety net against an IdP that lets users self-attest emails.
